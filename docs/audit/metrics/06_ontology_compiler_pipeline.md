## 4. ANALYSIS (labeled)

Vs enterprise schema pipelines (Protobuf/OpenAPI codegen): the difference
is the determinism contract. Typical generator setups drift because the
generator version/plugins/options live in nobody's lockfile and hand-edits
accumulate between regens. Here every input is pinned (blake3 pack
content_hash in ggen.lock, path-pinned packs) and replay is asserted, not
assumed: gate0's cmp is a graph-single-source + byte-replay proof — the
CI-diff check most enterprise pipelines never wire. Honest defect record:
determinism was BROKEN in practice — gate0 failed FM-PACK-008 (W1-04's
pack edit landed without re-lock; w2-15 shows a projection clobber needing
re-prove), repaired only by re-lock, and the guard was vacuous until the
absolute-path fix. Lesson, per the repo's own law: determinism is a
property of the gate, not the generator — an unrun gate is
indistinguishable from no gate. The gate is now wired (`make gates`
includes gate0), but lock and guard both needed a defect to get there.
